Privacy at Vexcom

Last updated November 4, 2026

This page covers this website, vexcomllc.com. Each of our apps has its own privacy policy that explains what the app collects and why:

  • Dart Keeper privacy policy
  • Battle Tees privacy policy

What this website collects

  • Visit counts. We use Cloudflare Web Analytics to count page views. It does not set cookies and tells us totals, such as how many people visited and which pages they read, not who you are.
  • Server logs. The site is hosted on GitHub Pages. Like any web host, GitHub receives standard request details such as your IP address and browser type when your browser loads a page.
  • E-mail you send us. If you write to us, we receive your address and your message and use them only to answer you.

This website sets no cookies, has no forms, and doesn't show ads.

What we don't do

We don't sell, rent or trade personal information, and we don't track you across other websites. Because we don't track you across sites, the site behaves the same whether or not your browser sends a Do Not Track signal.

Children

This website isn't directed at children under 13, and we don't knowingly collect information from them.

Contact

Questions or requests about your information: privacy@vexcomllc.com. Vexcom LLC, Vacaville, California.
