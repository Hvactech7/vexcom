Vexcom LLC is a small California software company. We build the apps that run the game night and the golf round: one for bar dart leagues, one for golf groups. Both keep score on everyone's device at once and keep working when the signal drops.

Dart Keeper

Free on the web · iPhone and Android apps coming

A scorekeeper built for the board at the bar. Set it on a tablet, tap the scores, and Dart Keeper does the math, the checkouts and the league paperwork.

  • 301 with double-in and double-out, 501, Cricket, Fat 6 and more
  • League nights with teams, standings and a report e-mailed after every night
  • A league can schedule its weekly night, and every board turns on and off by itself
  • Player stats built from each player's own turns: averages, highs and 100+ turns
  • Works with no signal at the board and catches up when it's back

Battle Tees

In private beta · iPhone and Android apps coming

Runs the side games for a golf group. Everyone scores their own ball on their own phone, and the standings update on every phone in the group, hole by hole.

  • 19 side games, each with its own rules page: wolf, skins, Nassau, Vegas and more
  • GPS yardages to the front, middle and back of the green, with wind and elevation
  • More than 11,000 U.S. courses in the course library
  • A live spectator view so friends at home can follow the round
  • Plays a whole round with no signal and syncs when the phones reconnect
